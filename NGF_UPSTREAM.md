# NGF Marketplace Upstream

Initial marketplace engine derived from:

OpenClassify
https://github.com/openclassify/openclassify

Architecture:
- modular classifieds marketplace
- listing engine
- categories and locations
- seller profiles
- buyer/seller conversations
- favorites
- offers
- reviews
- reports/moderation
- notifications
- promotions
- video listings
- admin panel

The upstream composer.json declares the project license as MIT.

NGF Productions branding and production modifications are maintained
separately from the upstream identity.
